<h3 align="center">Computational Astrophysicist :rocket: | Scientific Software :satellite: | Simulation Research :abacus: </h3>

I am currently the McDonald Institute Postdoctoral Fellow in the Astrophysics of Dark Matter at Queen's University. I received my PhD from the International Centre for Radio Astronomy Research at the University of Western Australia in 2026. I have also developed scientific software for radio telescopes.

I’m broadly interested in galaxy formation and evolution. In particular, I focus on the growth and impact of stellar bars in disc galaxies like our own Milky Way. I primarily do this using idealised simulations of individual galaxies and cosmological simulations of small volumes of the Universe, but I also occasionally dabble in the "real" Universe.
